COLLABORATION WORKFLOW

How CivicConnect Works

A streamlined 4-step pipeline that bridges community issues directly with academic research and industrial execution.

01
1. Crowdsource Challenges

Citizens, students, and NGOs submit local and national challenges with severity metrics and expected impact.

02
2. Propose Solutions

Innovators and student teams submit technical architectures, timelines, and resource budgets.

03
3. Partner & Mentor

Universities review solutions and offer lab mentorship. Industry leaders sponsor R&D and pilot implementations.

04
4. Track Impact

Multi-disciplinary teams track project progress, milestones, and deliver tangible community results.
