How it works

From problem identification to verified societal implementation.

01

Identify challenges

Citizens, students, and NGOs submit local challenges with location tags, context, and severity ratings.

02

Propose solutions

Student innovators and multidisciplinary teams submit technical proposals, resource requirements, and schedules.

03

Partner & mentor

Universities provide faculty guidance and lab facilities. Industry sponsors fund pilots and provide technical expertise.

04

Track impact

Project teams collaborate in dedicated workspaces to monitor milestones, test prototypes, and report real progress.
